# Release Content Checklist

This checklist tracks human-supplied content required before `v2` is merged to
`main`. Placeholder content is not production-ready.

## Required before release

- Final profile introduction.
- Selected career entries.
- Academic and certification entries.
- Verified testimonials, or an explicit decision to launch without
  testimonials.
- At least one real published essay, or an explicit decision to launch with an
  empty Essays index.
- Removal or continued non-publication of the temporary `foundation-sample`
  essay.
- Final review of demo descriptions.
- Final contact-page copy review.
- Verified external links.
- Any social or profile links that the owner explicitly chooses to add later.

## Current PR 6 content boundary

- `foundation-sample` is a draft and must not be treated as personal writing.
- The Essays index may legitimately show the empty state until real writing is
  supplied.
- The site must not invent biography, career, academics, testimonials, social
  profiles, location, availability, salary, or hiring preferences.
