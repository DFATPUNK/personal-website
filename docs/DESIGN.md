# Design

The V2 design direction is editorial, minimal, and MatX-inspired without copying MatX.

Use:

- a light background;
- dark text;
- restrained accent colors;
- subtle borders;
- clear section divisions;
- typography-first layouts;
- a desktop sidebar and compact mobile navigation.

Avoid:

- heavy card grids;
- gratuitous gradients;
- complex animation systems;
- invented content;
- oversized marketing-page composition for functional pages.
